The InkFlowzPrt. Journey...

It all started in 2018, when our founder, Sarah, decided that local businesses deserved high-fidelity prints without corporate markup. Armed with a single manual press, she began printing custom cards and flyers.

What began as small flyer runs soon expanded into full book binding, banners, stationery supplies, and a rapid xerox copy service. Today, we're a team of copy specialists who believe that every document deserves perfect alignment and color accuracy.
